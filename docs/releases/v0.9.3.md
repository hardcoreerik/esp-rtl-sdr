# esp_rtl_sdr v0.9.3 — RF band select, unplug recovery, V4/V4L band select

Release date: October 2, 2026. Patch release; the public API and profile identifiers are unchanged. The maintainer
selected version `0.9.3` and authorized the `v0.9.3` tag.

## What changed

- **The R820T2 RF mux and tracking filter now follow the tuned band** (Blog V3 and Nooelec SMArt v5, issue #25). The tune
  sequence the driver replays was captured on FM, so every tune left registers 17, 1a and 1b on the 90-110 MHz setting and
  the front end heard almost nothing away from FM. The 15-row band table, keyed on the LO the PLL is programmed to, comes
  from our own black-box PC captures of the vendor DLL (47 tunes up and down on a Nooelec SMArt v5 and a Blog V3c, both
  sides of every boundary). Six further boundaries were tested on both sides and showed no register change, so they are
  not rows. The issue was found and reported by David Coulson ([@davidcoulson](https://github.com/davidcoulson)); his PR #45
  pointed at the cause and informed our investigation, and the implementation is ours.
- **Blog V4 and V4L follow the same bands above 28.8 MHz** (issue #51). The V4 also toggles register 17 bit 3 at 85, 112,
  172 and 242 MHz (RF-keyed); the V4L does not. HF at and below 28.8 MHz is unchanged. The band row follows the LO that
  `run_tune` programs, so a ppm correction that moves the LO across a boundary selects the right row.
- **Unplugging a dongle mid-stream, a dongle that fails to enumerate, and oversized or short control transfers are handled.**
  A bounded unplug shutdown (EP0 window, endpoint flush, URBs leaked rather than freed under the host controller), a
  root-port power-cycle retry with back-off, control-transfer bounds, a short-read error and a bulk-IN descriptor check.
  The problem was reported by David Coulson (PR #48, from his fork's #26); his report informed the investigation and this
  implementation was written independently from our requirements and hardware tests.
- **Nooelec SMArt v5: every start after a manual gain failed on ESP32-P4** (issue #42), and **low-band sample rates** in
  225001-300000 Hz were reported at about twice the rate (found and diagnosed by David Coulson in #24). See the changelog.

## What was verified

- **Host:** six host suites pass, including replay of every captured band-sweep point (15-row table, 383 checks; V4 and V4L,
  400 checks) and the USB guard tests; the Nooelec runtime trace passes 7990 checks. CI: Linux and Windows host tests,
  truth/version hygiene and the full ESP-IDF 5.5.4 ESP32-P4 compile.
- **Tab5 (ESP32-P4), one site, one antenna, one unit of each dongle:**
  - Nooelec and V3c: 453.925 MHz peaks 44-45 dB over noise with the band select; FM unchanged.
  - V4: 230 MHz 9.3-9.4 to 13.0-13.5 dB over noise at 2.4 MS/s, same dongle and antenna with and without the change;
    118.9, 162.55 and 453.925 MHz unchanged. V4L: streams and tunes correctly with the change; its earlier result was
    neutral for signal-to-noise, and this release did not repeat that comparison.
  - Five unplug and replug cycles on each of the Nooelec, V3c, V4 and V4L: every unplug detected, re-probed and restarted,
    no reboots, no leaked URBs. Two events are recorded honestly: on the V4, one stream start in seven replugs returned a
    USB error and the next replug recovered; and on one V3c run the Tab5 froze after the Wi-Fi coprocessor's SDIO link
    failed during a replug and needed a reset. That run was not reproduced, and its cause is not established.
- **Not verified:** hubs, Tab5 operation for hours with this exact build, and any dongle or antenna other than the ones above.

## Provenance

Every register value and boundary in this release comes from our own black-box captures of the vendor DLL and our own
measurements; nothing is taken from another project's source or tables. Capture data, scripts and the Tab5 results are under
`docs/captures/`.
